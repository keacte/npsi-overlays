Getting Involved

From first fleet to regular pilot_
FLEETS REGULARFRIENDS GROWINGFC MAYBE ONE DAY

TL;DRThe short version

Show up regularly, join a few communities, be friendly and reliable, and volunteer for roles. The more you fly, the more names you know. Maybe you will lead a fleet one day.

01Make it a habit

  1. Pick a weekly slotChoose one or two evenings. Regular pilots are the ones who build friendships.
  2. Try different hostsNPSI.rocks lists over 20 hosts and roughly 30 to 50 fleets a week. Try several. About NPSI.
  3. Join their Discord serversEach host has a community. Say hello. People answer questions and invite you to fleets.
  4. Recognise namesSoon you will spot FCs and pilots you enjoy flying with. Follow them.
  5. Keep coming backConsistency is the best way to become a known face.

02Finding your communities

Browse the host directoryNPSI.rocks

See what each host flies, when, and which style fits you.

Join the main DiscordAnnouncements

Fleets and news are announced there. Join the Discord.

Follow partner groupsVariety

NPSI.rocks lists partners from many groups, so there is something for every style.

Watch streams and videosGet a feel

Watch a few fleets before you fly if you are nervous.

03Building friendships

Be friendly in comms
Say hello, say thanks, say GF.
Learn names
Using names makes you memorable.
Help new pilots
You were new once. Share what you have learned.
Thank FCs
FCs volunteer their time.
Be reliable
If you say you will be there, be there.
Add people in-game
Add pilots you like. You will spot them in fleet and on the calendar.

04Level up

FLEET 1

Show up, listen, follow the FC, enjoy it.

FLEETS 2 TO 5

Start recognising names. Join a host's Discord. Try different ship classes.

FLEETS 5 TO 15

Volunteer as a scout or D-scan. Give feedback. Help newer pilots.

REGULAR

You know the FCs, they know you.
