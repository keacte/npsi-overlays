TL;DRThe short version

NPSI = Not Purple Shoot It. Pilots in the fleet appear purple on your overview. Anything that is not purple is a valid target. It is public, open to everyone and has no blues and no strings: just content.

01The idea

NPSI is a style of public PvP fleet. Rather than being blue to everyone, you simply fly with whoever is in the fleet. If you can see purple, they are on your side. If it is not purple, shoot it.

PurpleIn fleet

Friends. Do not shoot, and protect them.

Not purpleEveryone else

Valid targets. The FC will tell you what to shoot first.

No stringsNo commitments

No standings, no corp application, no obligations. Just show up.

"Not purple? Shoot it!" and "No blues, no strings, just content!" is how NPSI.rocks describes it.

02Why people love it

Accessible PvPFor new pilots

You do not need to be an expert, or in a big alliance, to get into fights.

Meet peopleCommunity

You meet pilots from many groups and learn from different FCs.

VarietyDifferent every night

Frigates one day, battlecruisers the next, wormholes, nullsec, lowsec and more.

Real contentFun to watch

Many NPSI fleets are streamed and recorded. Plenty of pilots enjoy sharing fights.

03A short history

According to the NPSI.rocks About page:

APRIL 2018

FUN INC / Even Better With Friends is founded after Agony Unleashed ends. Its members share a love of PvP.

SEPTEMBER 2018

FUN INC begins running NPSI public roams in Syndicate.

EARLY 2020

Those roams grow into the NPSI Community Gateway.

LATER

Discord calendar integrations, the Fluffy announcement bot and the npsi.rocks website arrive.

04What NPSI is not

Not a corpNo membership

You do not join a corporation to fly in an NPSI fleet.

Not a free-for-allOrders matter

The FC leads. Following instructions is how the fleet wins.

Not for friends onlyOpen to all

Newcomers are welcome. The charter promotes inclusion, not exclusion.

Not a place for toxicityFly together, win together

The community charter is built around visibility, variety and inclusivity.

05NPSI vocabulary

FC
Fleet Commander. The pilot leading the fleet. Listen to them.
XUP
Say you are ready, with your ship, when the FC asks.
Staging
The system where the fleet forms up.
MOTD
Message of the Day. The fleet's notes, shown in the fleet window.
Doctrine
The set of ships and fits the fleet is flying.
Primary
The target everyone shoots first, called by the FC.
Tackle
Ships that hold targets down with scramblers and webs so the fleet can kill them.
D-scan
Directional scanner. Used to find ships nearby, even if not on grid.
Broadcast
Fleet signals like Align To, Warp To, Target and Need Armor.
SRP
Ship Replacement Program. Some groups replace lost ships. Not all do, so check.
Staging / Home
Set your home station to the fleet's staging system.
GF
Good fight. Say it after a fight, win or lose.
