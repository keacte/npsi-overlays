Surviving Your First Fleet

Prepare, fly, lose a ship, come back_

TL;DRThe short version

Right ship, correct fit, ammo, paste, drugs, drones, a PvP clone, home station at staging. Stay with the fleet, follow the FC, and when you lose a ship, say GF and reship. It is part of the game.

02Staying alive

  1. Stay with the fleetStay on grid and near your logistics. Lone pilots die first.
  2. Follow the FCAlign, warp and shoot when told, not before.
  3. Know your shipSpeed, weapon range, EWAR and drones. Know your limits.
  4. Watch the overviewKeep an eye on what is on grid and what is targeting you.
  5. Retreat when calledIf the FC calls a retreat, go. Do not stay behind alone.

03Clones and implants

Use a PvP cloneCheap

Wear affordable implants. Do not risk expensive ones.

Home station at stagingReship quickly

If podded, you wake up at staging and can rejoin.

Check jump clonesWhere am I?

Know which clone you are in and where it is.

Do not fly what you cannot loseRule 1

If it would hurt to lose it, leave it in the hangar.

04When you lose your ship

NPSI is PvP, so ships die. It is normal for every pilot, including experienced FCs.

  1. Stay calmDo not rage in comms. The fight continues.
  2. Say "GF"A quick good fight is the NPSI way, win or lose.
  3. Save your podIf your capsule survived, warp it to safety or follow the FC.
  4. Reship and rejoinFit a replacement and come back if the fleet is still running and the FC agrees.
  5. Learn from itLook at the killmail later. Did you stay on grid? Did you know your range?

05Replacements and SRP

SRP (Ship Replacement Program) means a group replaces ships lost in their fleets. Some NPSI hosts do. Many do not.

Check the advert
It says whether handouts or replacements are offered.
Do not assume
If it is not mentioned, assume you are on your own.
Ask early
If unsure, ask in the host's channel before the fleet, not during.
Fly cheap
If a loss would sour your evening, fly something cheaper.
Replacement rules are set by each host. There is no single NPSI scheme.
