TL;DRThe short version

NPSI fleets are public PvP roams open to everyone. Pick a fleet on the calendar, read the advert, fly the right ship to the staging system, join the host's comms, XUP when the FC asks, then listen and follow instructions. Losing ships is normal. Have fun.

01The 10-minute version

NPSI stands for Not Purple Shoot It. Fleet members show as purple on your overview, so everything that is not purple is fair game. No standings, no corp membership and no application: if you can undock in a ship that suits the fleet, you can fly.

  1. Find a fleetOpen the NPSI calendar and pick something that suits your ship and your timezone. Full guide →
  2. Read the advertStaging system, start time, ship or doctrine, and which comms and channel to use. How to join →
  3. Prepare your shipRight ship, correct fit, ammo, paste, drugs, a PvP clone, and home station set to staging. Preparation checklist →
  4. Join comms and XUPBe in the right channel early. When the FC calls for XUP, type it in channel. What to do on joining →
  5. Follow the FCListen, align when told, warp when told, shoot what is called. Etiquette →
  6. Give feedback and come backFill in the fleet feedback form afterwards, then book your next fleet. Becoming a regular →

03What actually happens

BEFORE

The FC posts the fleet. Pilots move ships to staging in the days before and join the community channel.

FORM UP

The FC announces formup, asks you to XUP, and sends invites. You join the fleet and read the MOTD.

THE ROAM

You move system to system, follow broadcasts, and fight what the FC finds. Expect short bursts of action between quiet gates.

AFTER

Dock up, say thanks, fill in the feedback form, and replace what you lost.

Fleets are mostly advertised up to a week ahead, so you can prepare at your own pace.
