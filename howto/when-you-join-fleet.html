GUIDE 08 // ON JOINING

What to Do When You Join Fleet

Mumble, fleet chat, broadcasts and following instructions_
COMMS LIVEMOTD READBROADCASTS WATCHED

TL;DRThe short version

Join voice, read the MOTD, find your squad, load your overview, and watch fleet broadcasts. Listen to the FC, follow instructions at all times, and keep voice chatter short.

01First five minutes

  1. Check you are in the fleetOpen the fleet window. Confirm your name is listed and your role or squad is set.
  2. Read the MOTDThe MOTD in the fleet window holds the rules, comms and objectives. Read it fully before asking.
  3. Join voice commsEach group lists its voice details. Mumble is commonly used. Join the right channel and test your sound.
  4. Open fleet chatKeep the fleet channel visible. Questions go in text, orders go in voice.
  5. Check your overviewMake sure hostiles are visible, and you can sort by distance.
  6. Wait for ordersStay docked or at the staging point until the FC says to undock.
The NPSI how-to says each group's page lists its voice comms details, so set them up and test them in advance.

02Voice comms (Mumble and friends)

Get set up early
Install and test your voice client days before. Do not leave it to formup.
Join the right channel
Follow the advert. There may be separate rooms for the FC, scouts and general fleet.
Push-to-talk
Use push-to-talk so you do not broadcast background noise.
Listen more than you talk
Silence helps the FC. Speak only when you have information to share.
Keep it short
Say what, where, how many. Then stop.
Stay muted when unsure
If you are not sure, stay quiet and ask in text.

03Fleet broadcasts

Broadcasts are one-click signals in the fleet window. They are how the FC and fleet communicate without voice. Learn the common ones:

Align To / Warp ToMovement

Do what it says, when told. Never warp to a broadcast without the FC saying.

TargetPrimary

The fleet is shooting this. Lock it and shoot if the FC has called it.

Need Armor / Shield / CapacitorLogistics

Pilots asking for repairs or cap. Logistics ships respond to these.

In Position / Hold PositionTactics

Tells the fleet to take their place or stay where they are.

Jump To / Travel ToTravel

Follow the route. Make sure you are in the right system.

Broadcast spamAvoid

Do not send broadcasts for fun. It confuses the fleet.

04Following instructions

  1. Align when toldAlign to the next gate or target as called. Be ready to warp.
  2. Warp on the FC's commandWait for "warp" or a clear instruction, then go together as a fleet.
  3. Shoot the called targetLock and fire on the primary. Do not pick your own unless told.
  4. Stay on gridStay with the fleet and within range of your logistics.
  5. Say if you cannotIf you lag, crash or lose comms, say so. The FC can adjust.
  6. Retreat when calledIf the FC calls a retreat, go. Do not stay behind to fight alone.
Follow commands at all times and do not backseat fly. If you want to suggest something, wait until after the fleet.

05Roles you can volunteer for

ScoutEyes ahead

Fly ahead and report what is in the next system. Very valuable.

D-scanWatch the edges

Run directional scan and call what you see.

Scanner / proberFind targets

Scan down hidden targets if you have the skills.

TackleHold them down

Scramblers and webs. Often a fast frigate role.
