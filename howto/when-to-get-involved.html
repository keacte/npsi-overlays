TL;DRThe short version

EVE time is UTC. Fleets are advertised up to a week ahead in EVE time. Convert with the clock below, check the calendar, and follow hosts whose times suit you.

01EVE time and your time

EVE Online runs on UTC, called EVE time. Fleet times are quoted in it. Convert to your timezone before you plan your evening.

02Typical regional windows

Broad guides to when each region tends to be active. Always check the calendar for real times.

RegionTypical window (EVE time)Notes
EUAbout 17:00 to 23:00Often the busiest window, with the most fleets to choose from.
USAbout 00:00 to 04:00Evening in the Americas. Many hosts run US-friendly slots.
AU / Asia-PacificAbout 08:00 to 12:00Fewer fleets, so following AU hosts helps.
These windows are indicative only, not published rules. Hosts set their own times and the calendar is the source of truth.

05Never miss one

  1. Convert the timeUse the converter above or add the fleet to your phone calendar.
  2. Scan the calendar for your eveningsOpen the calendar and look at the days you play.
  3. Follow hosts in your windowJoin the Discords of hosts whose fleets fit your timezone.
  4. Check again before you goTimes and staging can change. Re-read the advert on the day.
