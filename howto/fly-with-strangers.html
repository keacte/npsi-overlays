How to Fly With Strangers

Fleet etiquette, communication and expectations_
COMMS CLEAREGOS DOCKEDORDERS FOLLOWED

TL;DRThe short version

Be on time, be prepared, listen to the FC, keep comms clear, and follow instructions. Do not backseat fly. Be kind: everyone was new once, and the best fleets are the ones people want to rejoin.

01The golden rules

  1. Be on timeArrive early and in the right ship. Late pilots slow everyone down.
  2. Be preparedCorrect fit, ammo, paste, drugs, clone and overview. Do it before the fleet, not during.
  3. Listen to the FCThey are making dozens of decisions a minute. Silence your distractions.
  4. Follow instructions at all timesAlign when told, warp when told, shoot what is called. If you disagree, argue after the fleet.
  5. Read the MOTD firstMost questions are answered in the MOTD. Read it before asking.
  6. Be kindEverybody is here to have fun. Treat new pilots like you hoped to be treated.

02Do and do not

DoBe that pilot
  • XUP when the FC asks
  • Keep voice chatter short and clear
  • Call out new hostiles, system and distance
  • Volunteer to scout, scan or D-scan
  • Say "thanks" at the end
  • Stay on grid and with the fleet
  • Admit mistakes and move on
  • Offer help to newer pilots
Do notCommon mistakes
  • Backseat fly or argue tactics on comms
  • Talk over the FC
  • Break from the fleet to do your own thing
  • Spam chat or broadcasts
  • Blame others when a fight goes badly
  • Ask questions already answered in the MOTD
  • Rage when you lose a ship
  • Undock early or ahead of the fleet

03Communication

Listen first
Most of the time your job is to listen. Only speak when you have something useful.
Keep it short
Say what, where, how many. "Three hostile Caracals, on the Jita gate, 40 km." Then stop.
Use push-to-talk
Avoid open mics. Keyboard noise, pets, music and family do not help the FC.
Know when to mute
If the FC is calling targets, nobody else is talking.
Text vs voice
Questions go in text chat. Voice is for fleet information.
Be clear in text
Use the fleet or channel the FC asks you to use, not private tells.

04What FCs expect from you

ReliabilityShow up, stay

If you say you will join, join. If something changes, say so.

AwarenessEyes open

Know your ship's speed, range, EWAR and drones. Know what your role is.

FlexibilityAdapt

Plans change. The FC may switch targets or retreat. Go with it.

FeedbackAfter the fleet

Fill in the feedback form. It takes about two minutes and helps FCs improve.

NPSI.rocks charter values: variety, visibility, inclusivity, and "fly together, win together". Read the charter.

05When things go wrong

You lose a shipIt happens

Say "GF", keep your comms clear and do not blame others. More on losses.

You make a mistakeOwn it

Say it once, learn from it, move on. Nobody is perfect.

You disagreeWait

Raise it afterwards or send the FC feedback, not during the fight.

Someone is rudeDo not feed it

Do not reply in kind. Tell the FC or host quietly if it continues.
