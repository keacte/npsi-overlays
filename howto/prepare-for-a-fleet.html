How to Prepare for a Fleet

Ships, fittings, ammunition, clones and supplies_
SHIP FITTEDCLONE PVPHOME STAGING

TL;DRThe short version

Right ship, correct fit, ammo, paste, drugs, boosters, drones, a PvP clone, and home station set to staging. Do it all before the fleet starts. The checklist below saves your ticks in your browser.

01Interactive pre-flight checklist

Tick each one off as you do it. Your progress is saved on this device.

02Your ship and fit

Fly what the fleet wantsDoctrine

Match the advertised ship class or doctrine. A great ship that does not fit the plan is a bad ship.

Check your fit twiceBefore undock

Make sure everything is online, nothing is overheating and modules have charges.

Know the limitsYour ship

Speed, weapon range, EWAR and drones. Know what you can and cannot do.

Stay within your budgetAccept the loss

Cheap and cheerful. If a loss would sour your evening, fly something cheaper.

03Ammunition and supplies

Ammo
Load your weapons and carry spares. Check your damage type and range choice with the FC.
Nanite paste
Repairs damaged modules. Bring some.
Boosters / drugs
Temporary buffs. Optional, but check the advert if the fleet suggests them.
Drones
The right type and number. Check your drone bay.
Cap charges / scripts
If your fit needs them, carry spares.
Spare modules
Nice to have, rarely needed on a short roam.

04Clones and implants

When you are podded, your clone matters. Plan it before you undock.

Use a PvP cloneCheap

Wear affordable implants for fleets. Do not risk your expensive skill or training implants.

Home station at stagingReship quickly

If you are podded, you wake up at staging and can reship and rejoin the fleet.

Check jump clonesWhere am I?

If you use jump clones, know which one you are in and where it is.

Do not fly with what you cannot loseRule 1

If it hurts to lose it, leave it in the hangar.

05Timeline

7 DAYS BEFORE

Find the fleet, read the advert, decide on a ship.

2 DAYS BEFORE

Buy or fit the ship. Move it to staging. Set your home station.

1 HOUR BEFORE

Check the advert for changes. Install and test comms. Load ammo, drones and paste.

15 MINUTES BEFORE

Join the channel and voice comms. Overview loaded. Ship ready.

FORM UP

Wait for the XUP call. Read the MOTD. Listen.
