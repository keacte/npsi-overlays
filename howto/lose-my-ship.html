What Happens If I Lose My Ship?

Losses, replacement ships and expectations_
LOSS EXPECTEDMOOD GFREPLACEMENT VARIES

TL;DRThe short version

You will lose ships. It is normal. Fly what you can afford to lose, say "GF", and reship. Some hosts offer handouts or replacements (check the advert), but never assume. Losses are how you learn.

01Losing ships is part of the game

NPSI fleets are PvP, and PvP means ships die, on both sides. Nobody wins every fight. A loss is not a failure. It is how people learn and why the next fight feels good.

NormalExpected

Every pilot, including experienced FCs, loses ships regularly.

Cheap by designBudget fits

Frigates and cruisers keep losses small, so you can fly again tomorrow.

Part of the contentMemorable fights

The best stories usually involve someone exploding.

Learning opportunitiesRead the killmail

zKillboard shows what happened. Use it to improve.

02What to do when your ship dies

  1. Stay calmTake a breath. Do not rage in comms. The fight continues.
  2. Say "GF"A quick "good fight" is the NPSI way, win or lose.
  3. Check your podIf your capsule survived, warp it to safety or a station. Follow the FC if they give instructions.
  4. Get back to stagingIf you were podded, you wake up in your home station, which should be staging.
  5. ReshipFit a replacement and rejoin, if the fleet is still running and the FC is happy to have you.
  6. Learn from itLook at the killmail later. Did you stay on grid? Did you know your range?
Set your home station to the staging system so a pod-out puts you right where you need to be.

03Replacement ships and SRP

SRP (Ship Replacement Program) means a group pays for or replaces ships lost in their fleets. Some NPSI hosts do. Many do not.

Always check the advert
It will say whether handouts or replacements are offered. For example, some fleets provide ships for pilots.
Do not assume
If it is not mentioned, assume you are on your own.
Handouts
Some fleets give out ships at staging so you can join easily. Use them if you need to.
Ask politely
If unsure, ask in the host's channel before the fleet. Not during.
Follow the rules
If SRP exists, follow the process. Do not expect payment otherwise.
The NPSI how-to does not set out any universal replacement scheme. Replacement rules are set by each host.

04Reduce the pain

Fly cheapBudget

If you cannot afford the loss, do not fly it.

Plan aheadThink ahead

Keep a spare ship at staging and a spare fit saved, so reshipping takes seconds.

Keep a reship readySpare

Bring a second ship to staging so you can rejoin fast.

Follow ordersSurvive longer

Staying with the fleet and following the FC is the best way to stay alive.

05Handling the feeling

Losing a ship can sting, especially the first time. That is okay.

Be a good sportAttitude

A calm attitude after a loss is remembered by FCs and fleetmates.

Talk about itAfter the fleet

Ask what you could have done differently. People enjoy helping.

Do not blame othersAvoid

Complaining in comms hurts the fleet and your reputation.

Do not quitKeep going

Everyone who is good at this lost a lot of ships first.
