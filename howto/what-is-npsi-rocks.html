What Is NPSI.rocks?

The central hub for public EVE fleets_

TL;DRThe short version

NPSI.rocks is the central hub for public EVE Online fleets. It lists upcoming fleets from many hosts in one calendar, shows who runs them, and helps the community find, schedule and celebrate NPSI content.

01What it is

NPSI.rocks is the home of the NPSI Community Gateway. Instead of searching a dozen Discords for tonight's fleet, you open one site and see everything.

Calendarnpsi.rocks/calendar

Every upcoming fleet from every host. Open it.

HostsWho runs fleets

A directory of the communities that host NPSI content, so you can find a style you like.

LeaderboardsBragging rights

Pilot kill rankings, including weekly top lists.

NewsWhat is new

Announcements from the gateway and community.

02What you will find on it

Upcoming fleets
A list of fleets coming up in the next week, with host, FC, staging and time.
Roam reports
Results from past fleets, such as ISK destroyed and the FC who led them.
How to NPSI
A short new-pilot page. Read it.
Community charter
The values behind the site. Read it.
Discord bot
Fleet announcements are delivered through the Fluffy bot in Discord.
Becoming an NPSI FC
An application page for anyone who wants to lead fleets. Linked from the site footer.

03How it works

  1. Hosts post fleetsCommunities add their fleets to the central calendar.
  2. The calendar collects themEverything appears in one place, in EVE time.
  3. Discord spreads the wordAnnouncements and reminders go out through the NPSI Discord and the Fluffy bot.
  4. Pilots turn upYou pick a fleet, prepare and join. No sign-up on the site is needed to fly.
  5. Feedback closes the loopAfter a roam, pilots fill in the fleet feedback form, which helps FCs improve.

04The numbers

According to the About page there are over 20 NPSI hosts and roughly 30 to 50 fleets each week. Partners include F.U.N. Inc., EVE LinkNet, Spectre Fleet, The Morgue, Bombers Bar and more, so there is a wide range of styles.

The site is run as an EVE partner project and promotes variety, visibility and inclusivity of NPSI public content.
