Fleet Etiquette

How to fly well with strangers_
EGOS DOCKEDORDERS FOLLOWEDMOOD FRIENDLY
The golden rulesDo and do notWhat FCs expect from youFollowing instructionsWhen things go wrong

TL;DRThe short version

Be on time, be prepared, listen to the FC and follow instructions. Do not backseat fly. Be kind: everyone was new once, and the best fleets are ones people want to rejoin.

01The golden rules

  1. Be on timeArrive early and in the right ship. Late pilots slow everyone down.
  2. Be preparedCorrect fit, ammo, paste, drugs, clone and overview. Do it before the fleet.
  3. Listen to the FCThey make dozens of decisions a minute. Turn off distractions.
  4. Follow instructions at all timesAlign when told, warp when told, shoot what is called. Argue afterwards.
  5. Read the MOTD firstMost questions are answered there.
  6. Be kindTreat new pilots like you hoped to be treated.

02Do and do not

DoBe that pilot
  • XUP when the FC asks
  • Keep chatter short and clear
  • Call out new hostiles with system and distance
  • Volunteer to scout, scan or D-scan
  • Say thanks at the end
  • Stay on grid and with the fleet
  • Admit mistakes and move on
  • Help newer pilots
Do notCommon mistakes
  • Backseat fly or argue tactics
  • Talk over the FC
  • Break from the fleet
  • Spam chat or broadcasts
  • Blame others after a loss
  • Ask what the MOTD already says
  • Rage when you lose a ship
  • Undock ahead of the fleet

03What FCs expect from you

ReliabilityShow up, stay

If you say you will join, join. If things change, say so.

AwarenessEyes open

Know your ship's speed, range, EWAR and drones, and your role.

FlexibilityAdapt

Plans change. The FC may switch targets or retreat. Go with it.

FeedbackAfter the fleet

Fill in the feedback form. It takes about two minutes.

NPSI.rocks charter values: variety, visibility, inclusivity and "fly together, win together". Read the charter.

04Following instructions

  1. Align when toldAlign to the next gate or target as called. Be ready to warp.
  2. Warp on commandWait for the call, then go together as a fleet.
  3. Shoot the called targetLock and fire on the primary. Do not pick your own unless told.
  4. Say if you cannotIf you lag, crash or lose comms, tell the FC.
  5. Retreat when calledIf the FC calls a retreat, go.
Follow commands at all times and do not backseat fly. Suggestions can wait until after the fleet.

05When things go wrong

You lose a shipIt happens

Say GF and keep comms clear. Do not blame others.

You make a mistakeOwn it

Say it once, learn from it, move on.

You disagreeWait

Raise it afterwards or in the feedback form.

Someone is rudeDo not feed it

Do not reply in kind. Tell the FC or host quietly if it continues.
