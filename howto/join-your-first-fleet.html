How to Join Your First Fleet

Fleet adverts, fleet invites and joining instructions_

TL;DRThe short version

Read the advert, be at the staging system early, join the host's channel and voice comms, then wait for the FC to prompt you to XUP. Do not XUP early, and do not spam. The FC will send invites in order.

01Reading a fleet advert

A good advert answers every question you have. If it does not, do not guess. Ask politely in the community channel.

When and whereTime + staging

Start time in EVE time and the staging system. Everything else depends on these.

What to flyDoctrine

The ship type or doctrine the FC wants. Bring the right ship, not your favourite.

How to communicateComms

Voice server details and the text channel to XUP in. Each group lists its own.

Rules of the roamMOTD / notes

Anything unusual: handouts, area of space, ISK limits or required boosters.

02Joining step by step

  1. Check the advert the day beforeTime, staging system, ship and comms. If anything is unclear, ask in the community channel.
  2. Move your ship to stagingDo this in the days before, not five minutes before formup. Set your home station there too.
  3. Join the voice comms and the text channelTest your microphone and speakers. Join the channel the advert tells you to use. Join early and listen.
  4. Wait for the XUP callThe FC will tell the channel when to XUP. Type x or xup with your ship, for example "x, Caracal", as the host asks.
  5. Accept the fleet inviteThe FC sends invites. Accept the pop-up, then check you are in the fleet window.
  6. Read the MOTDThe Message of the Day holds the rules, comms and objectives for the roam. Read it before you ask questions.
  7. Undock or wait for instructionsDo not undock until the FC says so unless the advert says otherwise.

03What does XUP mean?

XUP means "I am ready to join, here is my ship". The FC uses the XUP list to see who is available, which ships are coming, and who to invite first.

FormatUsually

x, or xup, plus ship name, like x Caracal

WhenOnly on call

Wait until the FC asks. Early messages clutter the channel.

AfterBe patient

You may wait a few minutes. Stay in comms and keep an eye on invites.

04Troubleshooting

No invite arrived
Check you are in the correct channel and that your XUP matched the format. Politely ask the FC, once.
Cannot hear the FC
Check you joined the right voice channel and your output device. Ask in text if you cannot fix it.
Wrong staging system
Tell the channel. Experienced pilots will help you catch up, or the FC may wait.
Late to formup
Message the channel, say where you are and ask if you can catch up. Do not warp straight to the fleet.
Not sure what to fly
Ask which ship class fits the roam. Anything in the right role is better than the wrong ship.
Never be afraid to ask. FCs would rather answer a question than lose a pilot to confusion.
