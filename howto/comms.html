Comms

Voice, fleet chat and broadcasts_

TL;DRThe short version

Set up and test your voice comms before the fleet. Join the right channel, use push-to-talk, listen more than you talk, keep calls short, and watch the fleet broadcasts.

01Voice comms

Each group lists its voice comms details on its page, so set them up and test them in advance. Mumble is commonly used, but check the advert.

Get set up early
Install and test your voice client days before, not at formup.
Join the right channel
Follow the advert. There may be separate rooms for the FC, scouts and general fleet.
Push-to-talk
Avoid open mics. Keyboard noise, music and background chatter do not help.
Listen first
Most of the time your job is to listen.
Stay muted when unsure
If you are not sure, stay quiet and ask in text.

02How to make a call

Say what, where, how many. Then stop.

Good callClear and short

"Three hostile Caracals, Jita gate, 40 km."

Bad callVague and long

"There are some ships, I think, and I am not sure where they went."

Questions go in textKeep voice clear

Voice is for fleet information. Ask questions in chat.

Use the channel the FC asks forNot private tells

Keep fleet talk in fleet channels.

03Fleet chat and the MOTD

  1. Check you are in the fleetOpen the fleet window and confirm your name and squad.
  2. Read the MOTDIt holds the rules, comms and objectives for the roam. Read it before asking questions.
  3. Open fleet chatKeep the fleet channel visible. Questions in text, orders in voice.
  4. XUP in the right channelUse the channel named in the advert, and wait for the FC's call.

04Fleet broadcasts

Broadcasts are one-click signals in the fleet window. They let the fleet communicate without voice.

Align To / Warp ToMovement

Do what it says, when told. Never warp without the FC's say-so.

TargetPrimary

The fleet is shooting this. Lock it if the FC has called it.

Need Armor / Shield / CapacitorLogistics

Pilots asking for repairs. Logistics ships respond.

In Position / Hold PositionTactics

Take your place or stay where you are.

Jump To / Travel ToTravel

Follow the route and check you are in the right system.

Broadcast spamAvoid

Do not send broadcasts for fun. It confuses the fleet.

05Roles you can volunteer for

ScoutEyes ahead

Fly ahead and report what is in the next system.

D-scanWatch the edges

Run directional scan and call what you see.

ScannerFind targets

Scan down hidden targets if you have the skills.

TackleHold them down

Scramblers and webs. Often a fast frigate role.

06Comms checklist

Before the fleetDays before

Install the voice client, test mic and speakers, find the channel.

At formup15 minutes before

Join voice and text, read the MOTD, wait for XUP.

DuringRoam

Push-to-talk, short calls, watch broadcasts, follow the FC.

AfterWrap-up

Say thanks and GF, then fill in the feedback form.
