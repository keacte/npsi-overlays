How to Get Involved

Find a fleet, join it and fly_

TL;DRThe short version

Find a fleet on the calendar, read the advert, move your ship to staging, join the host's comms, then XUP when the FC asks. That is all it takes.

01The six steps

  1. Find a fleetOpen the NPSI calendar. Pick something that suits your ship and your time.
  2. Read the advertCheck staging system, start time, ship or doctrine, and the comms and channel to use.
  3. Prepare your shipRight ship, correct fit, ammo, paste, drugs, boosters, drones, a PvP clone. Checklist →
  4. Move to stagingDo this in the days before, and set your home station there.
  5. Join comms and XUPBe in the right channel early. When the FC asks, type x with your ship, for example "x Caracal".
  6. Accept the invite and follow the FCRead the MOTD, then listen and follow instructions.

02Reading a fleet advert

Fleet name
Hints at the ship class or theme.
Host and FC
The community and the Fleet Commander leading it.
Staging system
Where to bring your ship. The most important detail.
Start time
Given in EVE time (UTC). Convert it →
Ship / doctrine
What to fly. Some fleets are "bring anything", some are strict.
Handouts
Some hosts provide ships. Check, do not assume.

03What XUP means

XUP means "I am ready, here is my ship". It lets the FC see who is available and which ships are coming.

FormatUsually

x Caracal or xup plus your ship.

WhenOnly on call

Wait until the FC asks. Early messages clutter the channel.

AfterBe patient

You may wait a few minutes. Stay in comms and watch for invites.

04Your first fleet: picking well

Pick a friendly formatBeginner-safe

Frigate and cruiser roams are cheap, fast and forgiving.

Pick a time you can finishCommit

A roam can run a couple of hours. Do not join if you must leave soon.

Pick a ship you can afford to loseBudget

Cheap fits are a feature, not a flaw.

Avoid fleets you cannot reachLogistics

If staging is many jumps away, choose another fleet.

05If something goes wrong

No invite arrived
Check you are in the correct channel and used the right XUP format. Politely ask the FC, once.
Wrong staging system
Tell the channel. Experienced pilots will help you catch up.
Late to formup
Message the channel, say where you are and ask if you can catch up.
Unsure what to fly
Ask which ship class fits the roam. The right role beats your favourite ship.
Never be afraid to ask. FCs would rather answer a question than lose a pilot to confusion.
