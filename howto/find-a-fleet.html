How to Find a Fleet

Finding public fleets on NPSI.rocks_

TL;DRThe short version

Everything is on the NPSI calendar. Filter by your time, check the staging system and ship type, then follow the host in Discord to get reminders. Fleets are mostly advertised up to a week ahead.

01Where fleets are listed

NPSI.rocks is the central hub for public EVE fleets. It lists upcoming fleets from many different hosts in one place, so you do not need to hunt around.

NPSI calendarnpsi.rocks/calendar

Every upcoming fleet in one list. Open the calendar.

The homepagenpsi.rocks

Shows what is coming up in the next seven days and recent roam reports. Open NPSI.rocks.

Host directoryHosts page

Browse the communities that run fleets, learn who they are and what they fly.

Discord announcementsFUN Inc Discord

Fleets are advertised in Discord, often up to a week ahead. Join the Discord.

02How to read a fleet listing

Each fleet card tells you what you need to decide whether to fly it. Look for:

Fleet name
Often hints at the ship class or theme. Things like "Cheap Yeet" or "T3D Tuesday" tell you what to expect.
Host and FC
The community running it and the Fleet Commander leading it.
Staging system
Where to bring your ship before the fleet forms. This is the most important thing to get right.
Start time
Given in EVE time (UTC). Convert it to your timezone.
Ship / doctrine
Which ships you should fly. Some fleets are "bring anything", some are strict.
Handouts / support
Some hosts provide ships. Always check the advert instead of assuming.

03Choosing your first fleet

Pick a friendly formatBeginner-safe

Frigate and cruiser roams are cheap, fast and forgiving. A great place to learn.

Pick a time you can finishCommit

A roam can run a couple of hours. Do not join if you need to leave in 20 minutes.

Pick a ship you can afford to loseBudget

Never fly something you would be upset to lose. Cheap fits are a feature, not a flaw.

Avoid fleets you cannot reachLogistics

If staging is ten jumps away and you only have an hour, choose another fleet.

04Never miss a fleet

  1. Follow hosts you enjoyJoin the host's Discord or community channel so you see changes and last-minute notices.
  2. Set a reminderAdd the fleet time to your phone or calendar in your own timezone.
  3. Check again an hour beforeTimes and staging can change. Re-read the advert on the day.
Most hosts post a ping in their community channel when the fleet is about to form.
