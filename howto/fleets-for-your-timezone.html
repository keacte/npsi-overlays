TL;DRThe short version

EVE time is UTC. Fleets are announced in EVE time. Use the clock below to convert, then look for fleets that fit your evening on the calendar. Hosts tend to cover different regions, so follow a few.

01EVE time and your time

EVE Online runs on UTC, called EVE time. All fleet times are quoted in it. Convert to your own timezone before you plan your evening.

02Typical regional windows

These are broad guides to when each region tends to be active. Always check the calendar for the real times.

RegionTypical fleet window (EVE time)Notes
EULate afternoon to late evening, about 17:00 to 23:00Often the busiest window, with the most fleets to choose from.
USEvening in the Americas, about 00:00 to 04:00Quieter than EU but a strong community. Many hosts run US-friendly slots.
AU / Asia-PacificEvening in Australia, about 08:00 to 12:00Fewer fleets, so following AU hosts helps a lot.
These windows are indicative only and are not published rules. Hosts set their own times, and the calendar is always the source of truth.

04Finding fleets for your hours

  1. Convert the fleet start timeUse the converter above, or add the calendar to your phone.
  2. Scan the calendar for your eveningOpen the calendar and look at the days you play. Note which hosts run in your window.
  3. Follow region-friendly hostsJoin the Discords of hosts whose fleets fit your timezone. They post reminders you can see.
  4. Ask in DiscordAsk "any US-time frigate fleets this week?" The community usually knows.
  5. Be ready to adaptIf nothing fits tonight, look at tomorrow, or try a weekend. Weekends often have more variety.

05Tips for off-peak pilots

Plan aheadA week ahead

Fleets are advertised up to a week ahead. Pick the ones that suit you early.

Prepare in advanceStage early

Move ships to staging earlier in the day so you can undock on time.

Support smaller fleetsHelp them grow

Smaller regional fleets grow if pilots keep turning up.

Watch for pop-upsAd-hoc fleets

Some hosts run short-notice fleets. Notifications on Discord help you catch them.
